# Health & safety notice — Forge

Last updated 9 November 2026

**Forge is a general wellness app, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional care.** Calorie targets, training suggestions, readiness scores, gut-pattern insights, meal plans, protocols and AI answers are estimates and general information. They are not a diagnosis or treatment.

## Get medical help

- **Emergency: call 112.**
- **Finland, non-urgent medical advice: call the Medical Helpline 116 117** (Päivystysapu), or contact your health centre or occupational healthcare.

See a doctor promptly if you notice, for example: blood in your stool or black stools, unintended weight loss, persistent vomiting, difficulty swallowing, severe or worsening abdominal pain, fever with gut symptoms, symptoms that wake you at night, chest pain or fainting during exercise, missed periods, or an eczema flare that looks infected.

## Diet and weight

- Talk to a doctor or dietitian before major diet changes if you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under 18.
- Restrictive diets and elimination protocols (keto, carnivore, gut reset) can cause nutrient gaps. Raw milk, raw eggs and raw meat carry a risk of food-borne infection.
- **Eating disorders:** if tracking food or weight makes you anxious, or you notice restricting, bingeing, purging or compensating with exercise, stop tracking and reach out. In Finland, the eating-disorder association **SYLI (Syömishäiriöliitto)** offers peer support, or contact your health centre.

## Training

- Warm up, use weights you can control, and stop if something hurts. Pain is not part of progress.
- Get clearance from a doctor before starting hard training if you have heart, blood-pressure or joint problems, or are returning from injury, illness or pregnancy.

## AI coach

The coach is an AI system. It can make mistakes, and it doesn’t know your full medical history. Check important advice with a professional.
