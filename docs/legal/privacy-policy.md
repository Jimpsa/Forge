## 2. What Forge is

Forge is a fitness and nutrition app for tracking food, training, body measurements, wellbeing and gut health, with suggestions based on what you log. It is a general wellness tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## 3. Where your data is stored today

- **Android app and web app:** everything you log is stored **only on your own device** (app storage on your phone or browser). It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes it, so use Profile → Export backup to keep a copy.
- **claude.ai version:** if you use Forge inside claude.ai, your data is saved privately in your Claude account by Anthropic, under Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy. Only you can see it.
- **Accounts and cloud sync** are planned. Before they launch we will update this policy, tell you in the app and ask for your consent again where needed.

## 4. What data is processed

- **Profile:** name or nickname, sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level, goals and target settings.
- **Health and wellbeing data (special category data, GDPR Art. 9):** food and drink logs, body weight and measurements, workouts and effort ratings, sleep, energy, mood and soreness ratings, gut symptoms and stool type, skin/eczema ratings, menstrual cycle data (if you turn it on), gut-reset protocol progress, and notes you write.
- **Plans:** training programs and meal plans the app creates for you.
- **AI coach conversations** (claude.ai version only), stored with your other data.
- **Technical data:** app version, device type and recent error messages. These are only included if **you** choose to send a bug report.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party trackers, and we never sell or rent your data.

## 5. Why, and on what legal basis

- **Providing the app’s features** (logging, targets, suggestions, plans): performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Processing health data** to provide those features: your **explicit consent** (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), given in the app. You can withdraw it at any time.
- **Handling bug reports and support requests** you send: legitimate interest in fixing the app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Complying with legal obligations**, such as accounting once paid features exist: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. Without consent to health data, the app cannot provide its core features, so you can delete your data instead (see section 9).

## 6. Automated suggestions

Forge calculates targets, training suggestions, readiness scores and food patterns automatically from your data. These are suggestions only, never decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you (GDPR Art. 22). You decide what to do with them.

## 7. Services that receive data

Only when you use the related feature:

- **Open Food Facts** (Open Food Facts association, France): when you search the online food database or look up a barcode, the search text or barcode is sent to Open Food Facts. Their servers see your IP address like any website. No account or personal data is sent.
- **GitHub** (GitHub, Inc., USA): app downloads and updates are served from GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address and device information when you download or check for updates. **Bug reports sent as a GitHub issue are public**, so don’t include personal health details there.
- **Anthropic** (Anthropic, PBC, USA): only in the claude.ai version. Data storage and AI coach messages are processed by Anthropic under your own Claude account. When you use the AI coach, a summary of your logged data is included so the coach can give personal answers.
- **Sharing you start yourself:** if you share a weekly recap, shopping list or bug report through another app (messages, email), that app’s own terms apply.

Transfers to the USA (GitHub, Anthropic) rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, where applicable.

## 8. How long data is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete it in the app (Profile → Delete all data), clear the app’s data, or uninstall.
- **claude.ai version:** until you delete it in the app or delete the artifact or your Claude account. Anthropic’s retention rules apply.
- **Bug reports and emails you send us:** until the issue is resolved, then deleted within 12 months, unless you ask for earlier deletion.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** your data and get a copy (Profile → Export backup gives you all of it in a machine-readable file),
- **rectify** incorrect data (edit it in the app),
- **erase** your data (Profile → Delete all data),
- **restrict** or **object** to processing,
- **data portability**,
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Contact us at the email above for anything you can’t do in the app. We reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish supervisory authority:
**Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto)**, PO Box 800, 00531 Helsinki, Finland · tietosuoja@om.fi · www.tietosuoja.fi

## 10. Age limit

Forge is intended for people aged **16 and over**. It deals with diet, body weight and health, so we do not offer it to younger children.

## 11. Security

Data on your device stays in the app’s private storage. Connections to the services above are encrypted (HTTPS). Update packages are checked with a checksum before they’re installed. If you lose your phone, protect it with a screen lock. We do not hold copies of locally stored data.

## 12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will appear in the app. If a change affects how your health data is used, we will ask for your consent again.
