You stay in control

Round 1

Settings in Profile. "Reduce motion" works like the switch at the top of this page: states still change, but nothing moves. Sounds are off by default.

Guardrails built in: celebrations reward effort and habits (PRs, consistency, closed rings), never extreme calorie deficits or fast weight loss. Animations stay between 0.15 and 0.9 seconds and never block what you're doing.

Vibration needs one more manual install of the app file. Everything else arrives as a normal automatic update.
